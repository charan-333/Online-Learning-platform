# CAT previous-year questions: sources and current blocker

**Status (12 October 2026): CAT 100 contains no verified CAT PYQs.** The import, review and
labelling workflow is complete and tested (see [PYQ-IMPORT.md](PYQ-IMPORT.md)), but no source was
found that is both lawful to copy and checkable against the official paper and answer key. The 65
bundled questions are original practice, written for CAT 100 and labelled as such.

## What a usable source needs

A question can become a **verified CAT PYQ** only when an admin can cite:

1. the official question paper (year, slot, section, question number), and
2. the official answer key for that question,

and the platform has the right to reproduce the question text. Anything less is imported as
`reconstructed` and shown to learners as an *unverified historical question*, or not imported.

## Findings

Research was done on 12 October 2026 with web search and page fetches from the build
environment. `iimcat.ac.in` and `cdn.digialm.com` (the official CAT sites) could not be opened
from that environment, so statements below about what those sites hold come from secondary
reports and need confirming by someone who can open them.

| Source | What it offers | Licence / terms | Can it verify a PYQ? |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAT website, candidate login (iimcat.ac.in) | Secondary reports (Careers360, Collegedunia) say the question paper, the candidate's responses and the provisional and final answer keys are shown to each candidate after the exam, behind their login, and are not kept permanently. Collegedunia states that the IIMs do not publish past papers on their websites. | Not reviewed; the site could not be opened. Check the CAT terms before reusing anything downloaded from a candidate login. | Yes, if access and reuse are permitted. |
| [CAT 2024 advertisement](https://iiml.ac.in/hi/sites/default/files/upload/news/CAT_2024_Advertisement.pdf), IIM Lucknow (official) | Exam date (24 November 2024), three sessions, registration dates. | Official notice. | No: contains no questions. |
| CAT information bulletins on cdn.digialm.com (official exam host) | Search found bulletins for CAT 2023, 2025 and 2026; none for 2024. | Not opened. | No: bulletins describe the exam, not its questions. |
| [2IIM question papers](https://online.2iim.com/CAT-question-paper/) | Papers by year, slot and section for 2017 to 2025, with 2IIM's own solutions. | Page states "Copyrights © All Rights Reserved by 2IIM.com" and that 2IIM is not endorsed by the IIMs. It does not say whether the text comes from official papers or memory. 2IIM [states](https://2iim.com/unacademy-plagiarism.shtml) that it obtained a Madras High Court injunction against Unacademy over copying its CAT questions. | No. Do not import without written permission from 2IIM. Even with permission, its answers are 2IIM's, not the official key, so items would be `reconstructed` until matched against the official paper and key. |
| Other coaching and PDF sites (aglasem, toprankers, mbauniverse, testbook, cracku, careers360) | Host PDFs said to be past papers. | Licensing unclear; origin of the PDFs not stated. | No, for the same reasons. |

## What would unblock verified PYQs

Any one of these, followed by the import and review procedure:

- Written permission from the IIMs (the convening IIM for that year) to reproduce the papers and
  answer keys, with the official files.
- A licence from a publisher who holds the rights to official CAT papers and keys.
- For memory-based or coaching-site questions with the publisher's permission: import them as
  `reconstructed`. Learners will see them as unverified historical questions, never as CAT PYQs.

Until then the platform keeps working with the original practice set. PYQ-first selection in
practice, lessons, revision and mocks switches on automatically as soon as approved PYQs exist.
